Profile

Research statement summary

I am an interdisciplinary researcher at UCL specialising in urban redevelopment, urban governance, and socio-spatial inequalities. I was awarded a PhD degree in Planning Studies/Urban Studies on 28 Feb 2026. Currently, I am contributing to a major ChinaUrban project funded by the European Research Council (ERC), exploring the governance and socio-spatial impacts of a new round of urban redevelopment. In particular, I investigate the role of the state and its interaction with market actors and civil society and explore the role of culture and heritage. I have a proven track record in high-impact knowledge dissemination, including authoring peer-reviewed articles, books, and book chapters; providing planning consultancies for local authorities; producing university-wide pedagogical resources for students; organising academic workshops, presenting at (inter)national conferences; as well as serving as a regular peer reviewer for international academic journals in social sciences. I am fluent in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin with extensive experience in qualitative research methods. I commit to bridging the knowledge gap between the Global North and Global East/South by advancing the understanding of social and political transformations in China and beyond.
