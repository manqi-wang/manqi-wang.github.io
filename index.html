Profile

Research statement summary

I am an interdisciplinary researcher at UCL specialising in urban governance and urban inequalities within urban redevelopment. Following the award of my PhD in Planning Studies/Urban Studies (Feb 2026), I am currently a Researcher working on ChinaUrban, a major project funded by a €2.49M European Research Council (ERC) Advanced Grant. I have a proven track record in high-impact knowledge dissemination, including authoring peer-reviewed articles, books, and book chapters, as well as producing planning consultancies for local authorities and university-wide pedagogical resources for UCL students. As an active contributor to the wider discipline, I have organised international workshops and seminars, and have presented at more than ten (inter)national conferences. In addition, I serve as a regular peer reviewer for over 15 international academic journals in the social sciences. I am fully trilingual (English, Cantonese, and Mandarin) with extensive qualitative expertise. I commit to bridging the knowledge gap between the Global North and Global East by advancing critical understandings of contemporary social and political transformations in urban redevelopment.
